    You said you've saved $100,000 or more. That's the situation most Medicare advice wasn't written for. It's pitched at people with very little to protect, and for you the same mistakes can cost your savings as well as your access to doctors.

    Why this is worth doing before you join, not after

    • The first choice is the one you can't easily undo. In most states, the six months after Part B starts is the one time supplement companies have to accept you no matter your health. After that, they can ask questions and turn you down.
    • Penalties for missing a window usually last for life. They're avoidable when you know your dates, and expensive when you find out afterward.
    • Most free advice comes from someone paid by the plan. Agents are paid based on the plans they enroll you in, and that doesn't mean the advice is bad. It does mean it may not be fully centered on you.
