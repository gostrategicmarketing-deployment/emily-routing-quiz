    You told us you put money into an HSA. There's a Medicare rule about that: HSA contributions have to stop six months before you join Medicare. Put it on your calendar now and it stops being a problem.

    While you wait

    • Keep the coverage that lets you wait. Coverage through active work generally lets you delay Part B without a penalty. Coverage that isn't through active work usually doesn't.
    • Check again when something changes. Retiring, a spouse retiring, a change to your plan, or a change in your employer's size can each change your dates.
    • Know the rule before the day you need it. Most of the expensive Medicare mistakes come from not knowing a rule existed until the window had closed.
