Quick answers

Is the check really free?

Yes. It takes about two minutes, it's free, and there's no obligation. Your result appears on the next screen.

Are you going to try to sell me a plan?

No. We're not insurance agents, we don't sell plans, and we earn no commissions. That's the whole reason you can trust what we tell you.

Will it tell me which plan to pick?

No. It points out the rules that apply to your situation and the right next step. The right plan depends on your doctors, your medications, your state and your coverage, so we don't name one from a quiz.

What if I'm already on Medicare?

The check still works. It will point you to the right next step for someone already enrolled, including whether it's worth a review.

What do you do with my answers?

We use them to work out your situation and show your result. If you give us your email we'll send follow-up about it, and if you add a phone number, we may call or text about Medicare help. You can opt out anytime.
