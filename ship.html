With Medicaid, your best first stop is free help from your state

Medicaid changes how Medicare works for you, and the rules are different in every state. The people best placed to walk you through that are your state's SHIP counselors, and their help is free.

What SHIP is

SHIP is the State Health Insurance Assistance Program, a publicly funded program that gives free, one-on-one Medicare counseling in every state. If you're on Medicaid or have a lower income, it's often the right place to start, because the programs that can help with your costs vary by state.

Find your state's SHIP → You can also call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) for general Medicare questions.

Questions to bring with you

  • Which of my doctors and prescriptions are covered, and by which program?
  • Does my state have programs that help pay for Medicare premiums or drug costs?
  • What changes if my Medicaid status changes?
