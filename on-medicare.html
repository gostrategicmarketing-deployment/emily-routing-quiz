    Already on Medicare

    You're past the first big decision, so the question changes

    Once you're enrolled, the question isn't how to join. It's whether the plan you're on is still the right one for your doctors, your prescriptions and your costs.

    Why a yearly look is worth it

    • Plans change every year. Doctors come and go from networks, medications move between cost tiers, and premiums and out-of-pocket costs change, usually without much notice.
    • Your life changes too. A new diagnosis, a new prescription or a move can turn a good plan into the wrong one.
    • Almost nobody checks. Most people keep the plan they have because looking is confusing, which is a fair reason and an expensive habit.
